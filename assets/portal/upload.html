Firmware Upload (Dev Only)

⚠ DANGER — Firmware Reflash ⚠

This will overwrite the firmware on the device. NVS storage (WiFi credentials, settings) is on a separate partition and will be preserved. If the new firmware fails to boot, the device automatically rolls back to the previous version. Use only on development or test devices.

Upload a compiled OpenBarista binary (.bin) from a development build. Maximum 1.9 MB. Flashing takes up to a minute — don't power off the machine.
